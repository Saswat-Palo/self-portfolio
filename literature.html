Teri Khusi

Ekdin kabhi jo roz milte the
Aaj bass baten hoti hai
Tujhe dekh ke maine samjha
kambakht ishq kya hoti hai
Tujhse baat karke maine jana
Koyal ki cheh chehat kaisi hoti hai
Tere jheel si ankhon mai hum jo fase
Ehsas tab hua mohabaat hai sirf tumse
Gum nahi agar tu jo na kahe
Khusi isime hai ki tu humesha khush rahe

Thandi Subahen

Sardiyan darwaze par dastak de rahi hain
Phir se wahi thandi subahen aa gayi hain
Ruh par thand ki halki si parat hai
Hothon pe nami,
Dill mein kuch dabii si baat hai.
Band kamron mein chadar odhe
Kayi dekh rahe bade bade sapne
Kisiko apnon ka apna banna hai
Kisiko khud ka pyara sa ghar sajana hai.
Kahin na kahin, in sardiyon mein hi
Akelepan ka sabse gehra ehsas hota hai.
Kyuki chutiyon ke baad
Woh andar ka bachcha bhi bada ho jata hai.
Woh halki halki dhoop ki kiran se,
Woh hawle hawle hawa ki lehar se
Pyaar sabko hota hai, magar dil mein
Armaan dabaye hue har koi
Apni mushakkat mein khoya hua hota hai.
